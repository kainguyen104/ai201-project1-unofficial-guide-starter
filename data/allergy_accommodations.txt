Description: Bon Appétit/Furman dining page explaining café hours, dietary filters, allergy accommodations, gluten-free options, wellness, and contact information for food allergy support.

The Bon Appétit Furman dining page provides information about café hours, menus, dietary preferences, food allergies, wellness, and dining contacts. The page includes café information for Daniel Dining Hall, P-Den, The Paddock, Traditions Grille, Blend and Bowl, The Library Café, Bookstore Café, and Bell Tower Bistro.

Bell Tower Bistro is described as a “We Proudly Serve Starbucks” location managed in addition to Bon Appétit dining locations.

The menu system includes dietary icons and filters. The icon legend includes Vegetarian, Vegan, Made without Gluten-Containing Ingredients, In Balance, and Seafood Watch. Students can use dietary preferences filters to search for menu items that meet their needs.

The page explains that Furman’s dining services team is trained in food allergy awareness and offers options for students with food allergies and celiac disease. These options may include communication about existing menu items, modifications to menu items, special preparation of menu items, and access to special products as agreed upon with students.

Daily menus for each café can be accessed at furman.cafebonappetit.com. Menu items appropriate for many students with celiac disease are labeled as “made without gluten-containing ingredients.” Students can filter menus by dietary preference to make searching easier. Top-9 allergens are included in menu names or descriptions whenever possible. Students with questions about ingredients can ask trained managers or chefs on site.

For guests with food allergies, celiac disease, lactose intolerance, or other medically necessary diets, the page highlights Root and Stem as a café destination where meals are prepared without the use of top-9 allergens plus gluten, using whole foods. Daniel Dining Hall also has a station dedicated to products made without gluten-containing ingredients.

The Nook provides purchased breads, cereals, and baked goods made with ingredients naturally without gluten-containing ingredients or alternatives to traditional gluten-containing options. A dedicated toaster and individual condiments are also available at The Nook. The Nook is located in a designated area of the café to reduce the risk of cross-contact.

Students with questions about food allergy services or requests for food allergy accommodations can contact David McMillan, Resident District Manager, at david.mcmillan@cafebonappetit.com or 864-448-4448. Students may also contact Judy Bagley, Director of the Student Office for Accessibility Resources, at judy.bagley@furman.edu or 864-294-2320.

The page also describes Bon Appétit’s wellness approach. Bon Appétit focuses on simple, delicious food that supports long-term health. The page says Bon Appétit is bringing more plant-based foods to menus and emphasizes healthy cooking techniques.

The page states that Bon Appétit serves guests with food allergies and sensitivities safely in cafés every day. Chefs, managers, and dietitians work with food-allergic guests to create reasonable solutions that help them eat safely and enjoy their meals.

The page also connects wellness with sustainability. Bon Appétit describes sustainability as including the health of animals, workers, the community, and the Earth. The company highlights commitments related to sustainable food service, cage-free eggs, antibiotics in animal production, and farmworker rights.

Students can subscribe to Menu Mail to receive daily menus by email. The page notes that menu items may change before service, so students should check the website for the most current information.
