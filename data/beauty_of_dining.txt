Description: Student-facing Furman admissions blog post by Elijah Poritzky describing the dining hall, PDen, Paddock, meal swipes, food points, and student dining experience.

The Beauty of Dining at Furman is an admissions blog post by Elijah Poritzky, last updated February 2, 2024. The post describes Furman dining from a student perspective and focuses on three dining options: the dining hall, The PDen, and The Paddock. Elijah writes that each place is unique and has its own dining style that students enjoy every day.

The dining hall is described as the first and probably most popular place on campus. Elijah says he goes to the dining hall at least two times a day almost every day of the week. He likes the dining hall because it has many options, including foods from around the world such as pizza, pasta, stir fry, salad, and burgers. The dining hall also has gluten-free and vegan options.

Elijah says his favorite part of the dining hall is the desserts. He describes the desserts as amazing and says they change every day. He also mentions Furman’s famous cookies and says he can confirm that they are amazing.

The post explains that students can choose from different meal plans, each giving students a certain number of swipes per semester. Elijah says he personally has the unlimited meal plan, which allows him to go to the dining hall as many times as he wants throughout the year. The daily dining hall menu changes every day and almost every meal.

The PDen is described as another dining place that Elijah loves. He says the PDen is useful because students can get food from restaurants they would usually have to go off campus for, including Chick-fil-A, Moe’s, and sushi. The PDen also has a full market with snacks and drinks, including ice cream and other sweet treats.

Elijah says the PDen is great when he needs to grab something quick on the go or when he needs a snack between classes. Every student has food points included in their meal plan, and those food points can be spent at the PDen. Food points act as on-campus currency and can be used throughout the year, carrying over from the fall semester to the spring semester.

The Paddock is described as the nicest restaurant on campus. Elijah says The Paddock has nice dining options, including salads and burgers. These can be used as meal exchanges or paid for with food points. His favorite part of The Paddock is the weekly specials. He describes The Paddock as a good place to take friends or family.
