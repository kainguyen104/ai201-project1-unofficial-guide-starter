Description: Official Furman virtual tour page and transcript describing Daniel Dining Hall, student dining spaces, and dining options.

Daniel Dining Hall, also called the DH by students, is Furman’s main dining hall. The page describes it as a hub for healthy eating and a place where students can find a rotating menu of made-from-scratch cuisine. The dining hall includes vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free options.

Daniel Dining Hall was erected in 1958 and named in honor of Charles Ezra Daniel. It houses Dining Services.

In the video tour transcript, Micah, a senior health sciences major at Furman, introduces the dining hall. He says there are plenty of spaces for students to eat and plenty of lines for students to get food. He mentions several dining options inside the dining hall, including a Mongolian Grill, a salad bar, and a burger bar.

Micah says his personal favorite option is the international food station. He also mentions Blend & Bowl upstairs, where students can grab an acai bowl. The transcript emphasizes that the dining hall has many spaces for Furman students to sit and eat.
