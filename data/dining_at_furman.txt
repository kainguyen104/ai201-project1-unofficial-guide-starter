Description: Official Furman Enrollment Services page explaining the meal plan portal, meal plan changes, food points, board swipes, and meal plan requirements.

Furman Dining introduced a new meal plan portal system designed to be faster, more efficient, and easier to use. The Furman Dining Meal Plan Portal is located at https://onecard.furman.edu/. The portal provides students with meal plan details, plan usage, the ability to change their meal plan, and the option to purchase Add-On Food Points in one place.

Students can use the Furman Dining Meal Plan Portal to make meal plan changes for the spring semester. Spring meal plan assignments will be the same as the fall semester plan unless eligible students make changes through the portal. To change a meal plan, students log in with their network credentials, select Manage Your Meal Plan, choose Spring Semester, select Manage and Change, choose the plan they want, then continue and confirm. The updated meal plan rate is added to the student account in Workday within an hour.

The system change does not affect fall Food Point balances rolling over to the spring semester. Any rollover Food Points and Add-On Food Points balances are added to the student meal account in January. Students must have an active spring meal plan to receive fall rollover Food Points.

For the 2026-2027 academic year, Furman offers several meal plans. The Unlimited Plan includes 400 board swipes, 80 exchanges, 5 bonus guest meals, and $350 in Food Points. It is required for first-year students and available to all students. The cost is $3,774.

The 190 Plan includes 150 board swipes, 40 exchanges, 15 bonus guest meals, and $350 in Food Points. It is available to second-year, third-year, and fourth-year students. The cost is $3,246.

The 160 Plan includes 130 board swipes, 30 exchanges, 15 bonus guest meals, and $400 in Food Points. It is available to second-year, third-year, and fourth-year students. The cost is $2,947.

The 110 Plan includes 85 board swipes, 25 exchanges, 10 bonus guest meals, and $450 in Food Points. It is available to third-year and fourth-year students. The cost is $2,138.

The 75 Plan includes 60 board swipes, 15 exchanges, 5 bonus guest meals, and $450 in Food Points. It is available only to commuters. The cost is $1,761.

First-year students are preassigned to the Unlimited Plan. Second-year students are preassigned to the 190 Plan. Third-year students and older students are preassigned to the 160 Plan. Full scholarship athletes are required to have the Unlimited Plan. The 75 Plan is only available to commuters.

For May X 2026, students who are registered for a May X class and living on campus are required to have at least the 33-meal plan. The May X room cost is $585, meals cost $460 for 33 meal swipes, and the total is $1,045. A May X 44-meal plan option is available upon request for $615.

For Summer 2026, Furman offers a Summer-60 Meal Plan for $730 for a single summer session and a Summer-120 Meal Plan for $1,430 for both summer sessions. Students with questions about the new portal can email dining@furman.edu.
