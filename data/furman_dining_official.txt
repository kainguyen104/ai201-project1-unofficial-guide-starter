Description: Official Furman dining page describing Daniel Dining Hall, campus dining locations, sustainability, allergens, meal plans, and dining services.

Furman dining provides made-from-scratch meals and restaurant-quality food prepared fresh daily. The dining program emphasizes rotating global cuisines, sustainability, and community. Meals are described as a way for students to connect with each other, not just as a place to eat.

Daniel Dining Hall, also called the DH, is described as a central dining space where students eat, build friendships, study together, and spend time before or after classes. The menu rotates daily and includes themed nights such as Taco Tuesday, International Thursday, and Sunday Brunch. Chefs use seasonal ingredients and take student requests.

The DH includes made-from-scratch global cuisines that change daily, eight food stations, wood-fired pizza, dedicated vegan options, fresh ingredients from the campus farm, all-you-can-eat meals through the meal plan, comfort food, and house-made cookies.

Daily stations include Grate & Griddle, which serves burgers, chicken sandwiches, and daily specials; Near & Far, which serves rotating international cuisines such as Thai curry and Italian pasta; Stone & Sauce, which serves fresh pizzas; Breads & Spreads, which offers build-your-own sandwiches; Greens & More, which offers fresh salad options with toppings and house-made dressings; Kitchen Table, which serves Southern favorites and home-style cooking; Root & Stem, which offers creative plant-based dishes and a separate prep area for gluten-free and allergy-safe meals; Meat & Potatoes, which is located near Root & Stem for students with dietary and allergen restrictions who want animal protein; Butter & Sugar, which serves breads and desserts; and Global Market, which offers daily specialty items prepared on the Mongolian grill and made-to-order omelets for breakfast.

Furman dining highlights several features, including Food Recovery Verified sustainability leadership since 2017, eight dining locations across campus, farm-to-table ingredients from the campus farm, and made-from-scratch food prepared daily.

Beyond Daniel Dining Hall, campus dining locations include The Paddock, P-Den Food Court, Bell Tower Bistro, Blend & Bowl, Late Night at Blend & Bowl, Library Café, and Traditions Grille. The Paddock is described as a place for signature burgers and conversations with views of Furman Lake.

Furman dining emphasizes sustainability through Food Recovery Verified status since 2017. The program partners with Loaves and Fishes to divert surplus food from landfills to people in need. The Farm to Fork Program brings produce from Furman Farm to campus kitchens, and food waste returns as compost to support future growing.

Bon Appétit at Furman serves students with food allergies and uses protocols based on open kitchens and from-scratch cooking methods. The dining program refers to recommendations from expert organizations such as Food Allergy Research & Education. Students requiring dining accommodations or meal plan exemptions must submit an appeals form to the Student Office for Accessible Resources. Furman dining states that it accommodates food allergies, dietary restrictions, and cultural preferences through dedicated preparation areas, clear labeling, and its partnership with the Student Office for Accessibility Resources.

Furman describes the meal plan as more than food. The meal plan is presented as support for students’ academic journey by providing chef-prepared meals, made-from-scratch global cuisines, fresh farm ingredients, and convenience. Furman says the meal plan saves students time by reducing the need for grocery shopping, cooking, and cleaning.

The meal plan includes a professional culinary team, farm-fresh ingredients including produce from the campus farm, convenience, community connection, and health and wellness support such as allergen-free stations and dietitian consultations.

Beyond regular meals, Furman dining also offers catering services, Knight Bites Food Truck, and food gifts such as cookies and care packages. Dining Services can answer questions about meal plans, dietary needs, and recommendations for family visits. The OneCard Office handles meal plan questions. Daily menus and hours are subject to change, and students are advised to visit individual dining location pages for current information.
