Source: Student Survey/Interview Notes on Furman Dining
Date collected: June 2026
Description: Informal notes from Furman students about campus dining experiences, including convenience, wait times, food quality, dietary options, and first-year advice.

Student 1:
I usually go to Daniel Dining Hall when I have enough time because it has the most variety. However, during lunch, it can feel crowded, especially when many students come between classes. If I am in a hurry, I usually prefer a quicker option instead of sitting down for a full meal.

Student 2:
The dining hall is convenient because it is central and easy for first-year students to use. The food quality depends on the day. Some stations are good, but sometimes the options feel repetitive. I think students should check the menu before going if they care about specific food choices.

Student 3:
For quick food between classes, convenience matters more than variety. I usually avoid places with long lines when I only have 15 or 20 minutes. The best option depends on where my next class is, but I usually choose whatever is closest and fastest.

Student 4:
There are vegetarian and healthier options, but they are not always equally exciting. I like that there are usually salad or vegetable options, but I wish the vegetarian meals had more variety. Students with dietary needs should probably check the menu or ask dining staff.

Student 5:
As a first-year student, I think the dining hall is useful because it is predictable and easy to understand. It is a good default place to eat, especially at the beginning of the semester. However, students should learn the busy times quickly because lunch can get crowded.
